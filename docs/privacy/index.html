Privacy Policy

Typer / សរសេរ

Last updated 27 November 2026

In short

Typer collects no data. It has no network access, so nothing you type, pick or copy ever leaves your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting.

This policy covers the Typer input method and keyboard for macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone and Android, and the សរសេរ companion app on iPhone and Android.

What stays on your device

  • What you pick. When you choose a suggestion, Typer saves the Latin spelling and the Khmer word you chose, so it can rank that word higher next time. Switch off Learn from my picks in Typer Settings and nothing new is saved.
  • Settings. Your choices in Typer Settings or the companion app.
  • Clipboard history (iPhone and Android only). The last 10 texts you copied, so you can paste them from the keyboard. Each one is dropped after an hour. On Android the list lives only in memory and is gone when the keyboard restarts; copies Android marks as sensitive, such as passwords, are never added. On iPhone it is saved in the keyboard's own encrypted storage, clips you pin are kept until you remove them, and you can switch clipboard history off in the សរសេរ app or by not allowing Full Access.

In password fields Typer passes your keys straight through and learns nothing.

Where the files are: ~/Library/Application Support/Typer/ on a Mac, %APPDATA%\Typer on Windows, ~/.local/share/typer/ on Linux, inside the app on iPhone and Android. Android backup is turned off for Typer, so this data is not copied to Google's cloud backup.

Permissions

  • Android: Vibrate, only for the key-vibration options. Typer does not ask for Internet access.
  • iPhone: Allow Full Access is optional. iOS needs it for key haptics, key sounds and clipboard history. Without it everything else still works. Typer still never uses the network.
  • Any keyboard can see what you type while it is in use; that is how keyboards work. Typer uses it only to make suggestions on the device.

Deleting your data

Delete the folder above, or uninstall the សរសេរ app on iPhone and Android. Everything Typer stored goes with it.

Children

Typer collects nothing from anyone, children included.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its Khmer font from Google Fonts. Those services may log your visit under their own privacy policies. The site itself uses no cookies or analytics.

Changes

If a future version ever needs to send anything off your device, it will be off by default, ask you first, and this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
